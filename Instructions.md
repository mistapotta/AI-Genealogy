# How to use our AI Prompt in your AI service

## ChatGPT.com
- Under *Projects*, click the *+* button. (name and describe how you choose).
- Under *Project Settings*, in the *Instructions* textbox, paste the Markdown text. Then *Save*.

## Claude.ai
- Under *Projects*, click the *+* button (name and describe how you choose.)
- Under *Instructions*, click the *+* button, then paste the Markdown text. Then *Save Instructions*.

## Gemini.google.com
- Select *Settings*, then *Personal Intelligence*.
- Select *Instructions for Gemini*.
- Click *Add*, then in *What do you want Gemini to remember* box paste the Markdown text. Then *Save*.

## Grok.com
- Select *Projects*, then select *Add project*. Name however you choose.
- Click on *Edit* under Instructions.
- In the *Set project instructions* box, paste the Markdown text. Then *Save*. 

## Muse.ai
- Under Side Chats, click +.
- In the *Chat* window, type the name of the Chat Topic, then control-enter, then pase the Markdown text. Then press enter.  

## Perplexity.ai
- *Projects* - *New Project* (name and describe however you choose).
- Click on *Instructions*, then in Instructions section paste the Markdown text. Then *Save*.
